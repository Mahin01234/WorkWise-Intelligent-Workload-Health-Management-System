# WorkWise - Intelligent Workload Health Management System

Welcome to the **WorkWise** project! This repository contains the data collection form and related resources for our intelligent workload and health management system. WorkWise aims to help individuals and teams monitor, manage, and improve their workload and overall well-being through data-driven insights.

---

## 📝 Fill Out Our Form

We are gathering data to improve our system and better understand the challenges of workload and health management. Your input is invaluable!

👉 **[Click here to fill out the WorkWise Form](https://forms.gle/oeYZwNhJJWmt1oov8)**

> **Note:** The form should take only a few minutes to complete. All responses are anonymous and will be used solely for research and development purposes.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Collect real-world data on workload patterns and health metrics.
- Analyze the relationship between workload and employee well-being.
- Develop intelligent recommendations for balanced work and health.
- Provide actionable insights for organizations and individuals.

---

## 📊 Data Usage

The data collected through the form will be used to:

- Train and validate our workload-health models.
- Identify common trends and risk factors.
- Improve the accuracy and usefulness of the WorkWise system.
